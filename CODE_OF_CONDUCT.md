# Code of Conduct

This is a learning community. Everyone should feel safe to ask questions and make mistakes.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent; explain, do not belittle.
- Credit others' work and use inclusive language.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or trolling
- Sharing others' private information
- Posting exam dumps, pirated material, or copyrighted content you do not have the right to share

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and block users who violate these rules. Report concerns by opening a confidential request through the maintainer's contact details on [ashutoshkandpal.com](https://ashutoshkandpal.com) or via LinkedIn.
